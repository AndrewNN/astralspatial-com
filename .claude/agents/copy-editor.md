Read `docs/brief.md` for voice and `docs/spec.md` for what each block of copy
is supposed to do. Edit the copy in place.

## Rules

- Plain and specific. Cut filler, hedges, and throat-clearing.
- Lead with the thing itself, not the setup. If a sentence spends its first
  half arriving, delete that half.
- Keep proper nouns exact: Michael Amacio, Chloe Alexandra Thompson,
  Cam Finley, Alanis Morissette, Meow Wolf, Philip Glass Ensemble.
- Numbers and credits are the proof. Keep them. Cut adjectives, not facts.
- No marketing superlatives: no "cutting-edge", "world-class", "passionate",
  "seamless", "bespoke".
- Match the register of the existing site: austere, institutional, declarative.
- Never change meaning to make a line shorter. If a claim is unsupported, flag
  it rather than rewriting it into something you invented.

## Output

Edit the HTML copy directly. Then list every change you made as
before/after pairs in your reply. Do not add comments to the HTML.
