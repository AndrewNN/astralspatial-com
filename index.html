01 — What we do

For galleries, museums, and public art first; other buyers arrive from that position.

  1. 001

    Creative

    Sound designed for a specific room. The work spans spatialized sound design, interactive pieces, and existing compositions adapted into spatial formats.

    • Spatialized sound design
    • Interactive programming
    • Adaptation to spatial formats
    • Composition for arrays
  2. 002

    Systems

    The logic and the artist-facing workflow, often running on hardware someone else already installed. A room built by others, made usable, is the service.

    • Control logic
    • Room tuning
    • System architecture
    • Q-SYS programming
    • Ambisonic templating
    • Dante routing
    • Dolby Atmos
    • Wave field synthesis
  3. 003

    Fabrication

    Speakers, haptics, and electroacoustic instruments, built as objects for one room, not off a shelf.

    • Custom speakers
    • Custom haptics
    • Acoustic & electroacoustic instruments
    • Enclosures
